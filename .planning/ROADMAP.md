# Roadmap: U2Tool

## v0.0.7 Organic Authority Re-Expansion

**Milestone:** v0.0.7 Organic Authority Re-Expansion

This milestone resumes growth after the `v0.0.6` trust-hardening cycle. The next cycle should reclaim natural-traffic momentum by selecting the next highest-yield unsupported authority/discovery gaps already latent in the catalog, shipping those surfaces from shared localized truth, promoting them across browse and AI discovery entry points, and governing the new growth layer through the canonical production gate.

Status: milestone opened on 2026-04-06; Phase 20 completed on 2026-04-06 by selecting the `text` authority wave and implementing shared localized source truth; Phase 21 completed on 2026-04-06 by promoting that wave across browse, compare, AI discovery, `llms.txt`, and tools-index exports; Phase 22 completed on 2026-05-04 by adding production growth-surface governance, clearing stale HTML cache, and restoring the full production QA contract; Phase 23 plans executed on 2026-05-09 by classifying GSC Coverage drilldowns, splitting Performance losses, and patching the confirmed Gantt title-brand defect.

### Phase 20: Authority Gap Prioritization and Shared Source Truth
**Goal**: Select the next highest-yield unsupported authority wave and encode it in shared localized source-truth structures instead of ad hoc copy additions.
**Depends on**: Archived v0.0.6 baseline
**Requirements**: AUTH-03
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. The selected wave is justified by concrete existing-catalog gaps and natural-traffic leverage, not generic expansion pressure.
  2. The new authority content model is reusable across locales and entry surfaces.
  3. The chosen wave sharpens topical precision instead of bloating already-covered areas.
**Plans**: 2 plans

Plans:
- [x] 20-01: Map unsupported authority/entity gaps and select the next highest-yield wave
- [x] 20-02: Implement shared localized source-truth and first authority surfaces for the selected wave

### Phase 21: Discovery Promotion and Internal-Link Expansion
**Goal**: Promote the new authority wave across browse, compare, AI discovery, and machine-readable exports so the new surfaces actually receive traffic and citation opportunities.
**Depends on**: Phase 20
**Requirements**: AUTH-04, DISC-03
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. New authority surfaces are reachable from the site’s main browse and comparison entry points through stable localized routes.
  2. AI discovery exports and snippets stay aligned to the same source-truth and keyword framing.
  3. Internal links, schema, and discovery snippets clarify intent boundaries instead of duplicating existing pages.
**Plans**: 2 plans

Plans:
- [x] 21-01: Extend browse, category, and compare surfaces to route traffic into the new authority wave
- [x] 21-02: Promote the same wave through AI discovery, `llms.txt`, tools-index exports, and machine-readable snippets

### Phase 22: Growth-Surface Governance Expansion
**Goal**: Add representative rendered/exported governance for the new authority/discovery wave so organic-growth additions cannot bypass the current trust baseline.
**Depends on**: Phase 21
**Requirements**: OPS-08
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Representative rendered and exported growth surfaces fail when they drift off-topic, leak locale residue, or lose discovery alignment.
  2. The canonical production gate remains one coherent release contract after the growth wave lands.
  3. Health and traceability artifacts stay aligned to the new authority/discovery evidence.
**Plans**: 2 plans

Plans:
- [x] 22-01: Add rendered/exported governance for representative new authority and discovery surfaces
- [x] 22-02: Revalidate milestone evidence and preserve zero-gap traceability after the growth-governance expansion lands

### Phase 23: GSC Evidence Recovery Triage
**Goal**: Convert the real GSC Coverage and Performance exports into a prioritized recovery queue, then patch only systematic crawl, canonical, noindex, 4xx, content-depth, or CTR patterns proven by URL/query evidence.
**Depends on**: Phase 22
**Requirements**: OPS-09
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. Coverage drilldown URL samples are classified by issue, locale, page type, host/canonical pattern, and recommended action before any broad routing or SEO copy changes.
  2. Performance page/query exports split the traffic loss into impressions, CTR, clicks, and average-position movement so we know whether exposure, snippet appeal, or page demand is the active bottleneck.
  3. Any code/content patch traces back to a confirmed recurring GSC pattern and passes the production SEO validation stack before deployment.
**Plans**: 2 plans

Plans:
- [x] 23-01: Classify GSC Coverage URL drilldowns and identify systematic crawl/canonical/noindex/4xx patterns
- [x] 23-02: Split GSC Performance loss by page/query and patch only proven exposure or CTR bottlenecks
