- **TDK warning evidence is now reviewable**: release output no longer requires

- **Root/base metadata stayed synchronized**: edited pairs were updated in both
  legacy metadata layers to avoid reintroducing drift between source layers.

- **Most TDK warnings remain**: `2792` warning-only findings are still present,
  dominated by overlong `seo_description` entries in `ja`, `ko`, `ru`, `fr`,
  and `ar`.

  stay small enough to review for topical accuracy rather than relying on
  automatic truncation.
- **Validator thresholds are unchanged**: this milestone improves signal and
  trims examples; it does not revisit whether current locale length bounds are
  ideal.
